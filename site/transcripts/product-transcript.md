# Scheme: your working computer, from any screen

Narration generated locally. Footage uses clean demonstration machines and fictional example content.

## 00:00 — Your work has a home.

Your work has a home. Your screen does not have to. Meet Scheme: a small, self-hosted cockpit for the computer that does the work.

## 00:08 — One working computer.

Your projects and terminal sessions stay on your working computer. Open a browser on another desktop, a laptop, or a phone, and reconnect through your private connection.

## 00:18 — Start with a simple hello.

Start with Shell. Open a terminal, run a command, and see the result. This is the real application running on a clean desktop machine.

## 00:27 — A place for each thread.

Keep separate tasks in separate tabs. Choose Shell, Claude Code, Codex, or a local model when those tools are installed. Your own accounts and hardware power the tools you choose.

## 00:37 — Find your way back.

Find a session, return to its project, and follow the activity your tools report. Missing information stays unknown, so the dashboard does not pretend to know more than it does.

## 00:48 — Close the screen.

Close the browser and let the host keep working. Come back to the same terminal. We tested that on two independent desktop machines, including a job that finished while the browser was closed.

## 00:59 — Make room to think.

Change the theme. Make room for the terminal. Use the smaller-screen controls when space is tight. The same workspace adapts to the screen in front of you.

## 01:08 — Keep the host awake.

Keep the working computer powered on and awake. A reboot ends running processes, even when saved tabs reopen. A private connection protects access to the computer that holds your work.

## 01:20 — Scheme

Scheme. Your working computer, from any screen. Open the setup guide, watch the full walkthrough, and try your first terminal.
